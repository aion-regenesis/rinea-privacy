Privacy Policy — Rinéa

Last updated: [ПОДСТАВЬ ТУ ЖЕ ДАТУ, ЧТО И В РУССКОЙ ВЕРСИИ]

This app is developed by a single independent developer ("we", "us", "our" below refers to the developer of Rinéa, not a company). We take your privacy seriously, so this policy is written in plain language, without legal loopholes — it describes exactly what the app does and does not do.

The short version. Rinéa stores all your data only on your device. We have no server, no user database, no analytics, no ad SDKs, and no tracking of your activity. We are physically unable to see your posts, sketches, photos, or notes — the app doesn't even have the ability to send anything over the internet (network access permission is explicitly excluded from the app at a technical level, not just as a promise in this text).

What data the app stores, and where

Everything you create in the app — posts, drafts, hashtag sets, Idea notes, sketches, reminders, settings — is stored locally on your device, in the app's private storage. We do not collect or see this data in any form.

Access to your photo gallery

The app requests access to your photo gallery (the READ_MEDIA_IMAGES permission on Android 13+, READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE on older versions) so you can pick existing photos for your posts. The app does not copy or upload the photo files themselves — it only stores a reference to the photo in your device's system gallery (the same place the photo already lives). If you delete a photo from your gallery, the reference in the app will stop working.

Notifications and reminders

The app requests permission to show notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS) and to schedule exact alarms (SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM) — this is used exclusively for the "Remind me to post" feature, which you configure yourself inside the app. Reminders are scheduled and triggered entirely on your device, with no involvement from any external service.

Android backup

Starting with Android 6.0, the operating system may automatically include app data in standard device backups to your own personal Google Drive account (this is a built-in feature of Android itself, present in nearly every app, not specific to Rinéa). If this feature is enabled on your device, Rinéa's data (posts, sketches, settings) may be included in such a backup. This happens through Android itself — we have no access to this backup and receive no data from it. You can manage this feature in your Android device settings independently of Rinéa.

What the app does not do

  • Does not collect usage analytics
  • Does not show ads or use advertising SDKs
  • Does not require an account or sign-in
  • Does not send any data to a server — the app has no operating-system-level permission to access the internet
  • Does not share or sell data to third parties — because it does not collect any in the first place

Children

Rinéa is not intended for children under 13 and does not collect any data from users of any age.

Changes to this policy

If the app's functionality changes in a way that affects data handling (for example, a new feature requiring internet access is added), we will update this policy and note the new date at the top of the document. We recommend checking the current version at this same link from time to time.
